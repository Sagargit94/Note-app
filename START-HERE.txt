PhysioNotes - quick start (no technical knowledge needed)

TO USE ON YOUR PHONE AND LAPTOP
1. Sign in (free) at https://app.netlify.com
2. Click "Add new project" -> "Deploy manually".
3. Drag the folder called  ready-to-upload  (it is inside this folder) onto the page.
   Do NOT drag this whole folder - only "ready-to-upload".
4. Netlify gives you a web address ending in .netlify.app. Open it on your phone and laptop.

TO TRY IT ON THIS COMPUTER ONLY
Double-click start.bat (keep the black window open while you use the app).
